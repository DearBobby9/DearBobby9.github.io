## Overview

EmboDi (Embodied Director) is an open-source mixed reality system for directing 3D scenes in a physical space. Creators place objects and actors, shape actions and motion paths, rehearse a scene, and record it with virtual cameras.

The workflow makes spatial relationships visible during authoring. Creators can work at room scale to arrange a story and adjust its timing before generating the final video appearance.

## Direct a Scene

1. **Build the scene.** Place actors and props in the room to establish positions, scale, and relationships.
2. **Direct the action.** Specify character actions and motion paths within the scene.
3. **Rehearse and revise.** Play the scene, observe how actions unfold, and adjust the staging or timing.
4. **Frame and record.** Position virtual cameras and capture the scene for the video creation workflow.
